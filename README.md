# Steam Roulette 🎰

> Play random games from your Steam library with an interactive 3D card deck!

Can't decide what to play next? **Steam Roulette** picks a random game from your Steam library or custom collections with smooth 3D card shuffling and casino sound effects.

---

## Features

- **3D Card Deck**: Displays games fanned out in 3D.
- **Shuffle Action**: Rapid casino card riffle shuffle with cycling cover art.
- **Sound Effects**: Card slides, riffle flutter, and winner chime (can be muted with 1 click).
- **Custom Decks**: Pick from All Games, Favorites, or any of your custom Steam collections.
- **Quick Filters**: Only show Installed or Unplayed games.
- **1-Click Launch**: Launch or install your winning game right away.

---

## Installation

1. Download **`steam-roulette.star`** from [Releases](https://github.com/mrgee-host/steam-roulette/releases).
2. Place `steam-roulette.star` into your Millennium plugins folder:
   ```text
   C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\millennium\plugins\
   ```
3. Restart Steam (or press `Ctrl + R`).
4. Click the **🎰 Steam Roulette** button in the bottom-right corner of your Library to play!
